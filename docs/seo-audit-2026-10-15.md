# SEO Audit: unpeeldesign.com

Date: 2026-10-15
Business type: Agency / freelance design studio with local-service signals (Ghent, BE). Service-area business, no storefront.
Scope: 34 sitemap URLs (17 EN/NL pairs), robots, sitemap, headers, asset weight, browser timing, schema, content, GEO, agent readiness, SXO, local.
Method note: the claude-seo managed runtime was not installed, so bundled scripts were not used. Checks ran with curl, stdlib Python, the in-app browser, and seven parallel subagents. PageSpeed Insights was unavailable (daily quota exhausted), so performance is lab-measured in a desktop browser plus asset analysis, not CrUX field data.

## What is working

- All 34 URLs return 200, self-referencing canonicals, reciprocal hreflang en / nl-BE / x-default, matching html lang.
- Unique titles and descriptions on every page, one H1 per page.
- Content is in raw HTML; no JS rendering dependency.
- robots.txt is open, sitemap valid, HSTS present, Brotli on text assets, TTFB 0.3 to 0.9 s.
- Schema on 32 of 34 pages: ProfessionalService, Person, CreativeWork, BreadcrumbList, CollectionPage, ContactPage.
- Contact form is a real POST form with labels and a submit button.
- NL copy is a full translation with Dutch titles that target "huisstijl" and "Gent".

## Findings by priority

### Critical

1. **No off-site entity presence.** No Google Business Profile evident, no Sortlist, Clutch, Behance, Dribbble, Trustoo, openingsuren.vlaanderen, or Bing index entry for the brand. A Malt profile (€350/day) outranks the site for "freelance brand designer Ghent". SERPs for every target query are dominated by directories and freelancer-marketplace profiles, so the homepage alone cannot rank. (SXO, Local, GEO)
2. **Relative image URLs in JSON-LD, og:image and twitter:image on every page.** Homepage uses `uploads/works/...png`, NL pages `../uploads/...`, case studies `../../uploads/...`. Invalid for Google rich results and social previews. Fix at template level with absolute URLs. (Schema)
3. **Testimonial and case-study credibility.** A Dutch quote is attributed to "Universal Music" with no matching case study and no person name. Melabody is described as "conceptual" on its case page but carries a client-voice testimonial on the homepage. No testimonial names a person or role. (Content)
4. **Homepage weight ~22 MB.** Hero video `Unpeel-Brandvideo-Web.mp4` is 10.6 MB with `autoplay preload="auto"` and no poster. Six work-grid videos add 11.5 MB, all autoplay. Browser first paint measured at 15.4 s on desktop. This is an LCP and mobile-data failure. (Performance)

### High

5. **No security headers.** Missing CSP, X-Content-Type-Options, X-Frame-Options / frame-ancestors, Referrer-Policy, Permissions-Policy. HSTS lacks includeSubDomains and preload. Fix in `netlify.toml` or `_headers`. (Technical)
6. **No long-lived caching.** Every asset including CSS, JS, images and the MP4s returns `cache-control: public,max-age=0,must-revalidate`. Fingerprint assets and set `max-age=31536000, immutable` on `/uploads/*`, CSS and JS. (Technical)
7. **Price and timeline contradictions.** /packages FAQ: "from around €1.000" then "between €1.000 and €3.000+"; tier cards show no price while NL meta promises "transparante prijzen". FAQ says 2 to 4 weeks (4 to 6 for a full system), tier cards and /approach say 6 to 8 weeks. AI engines and buyers will quote whichever they find first. (Content, GEO, SXO)
8. **Two different service lists.** Homepage: Strategy, Identity, Web, Packaging, AI Video (5). /services H1: "Four disciplines" with Branding, Web, Motion, "Artificial Design". Schema lists 4 services without Packaging. "Artificial Design" has no search demand. (Content, GEO)
9. **H1s carry no keyword on any core page.** "Creating brands that feel honest and last." / "Four disciplines. One coherent vision." / "Find your package." / "How I work." Titles are well targeted, H1s and opening copy do not support them. Same on NL: "Merken die eerlijk aanvoelen en blijven." (Content, SXO)
10. **No Dutch service landing pages.** "huisstijl ontwerper Gent", "logo ontwerp Gent", "branding horeca Gent" only exist as meta descriptions or H2s inside /nl/services. (SXO, Local, Content)
11. **NL schema points to EN URLs.** /nl/works CollectionPage and NL CreativeWork blocks use EN `url` and `@id`, and NL breadcrumbs carry English labels and EN URLs. Same `@id` with different-language content is a conflicting entity. (Schema)
12. **#business and Person declared three times with divergent properties.** Homepage, /services (with `url: /services`) and /contact each re-declare `#business`. Define once, reference elsewhere by `{"@id": ...}`. (Schema)
13. **Phone not on the homepage or footer.** Visible only on /contact. No tel: link site-wide, no NAP footer block. (Local)
14. **Service accordions are not operable controls.** `<div class="acc-header">` with no button, aria-expanded or role. Keyboard users and AI agents cannot reliably expand them. (Agentic)
15. **Location contradiction on /works/tuillerien.** Facts block says Knokke, body says Bruges. (Content)

### Medium

16. `.html` duplicates return 200 (`/index.html`, `/works.html`, `/nl/index.html`, `/works/smashed.html`). Add 301s to clean paths. (Technical)
17. No `width`/`height` on images; CLS risk once videos and images load. (Technical)
18. /services and /packages cannibalise: near-duplicate FAQs ("branding vs logo design" vs "logo vs brand identity"), "Do you work outside Ghent?" repeated on three pages, /nl/packages asks the price question twice. (Content)
19. /works is a 71-word grid with no intro copy; homepage links only 6 of 9 projects, so stellar-secrets and walter-swennen have 2 inlinks each. (Content, Technical)
20. Gmail contact address instead of a domain address; footer says ©2025; privacy page "Last updated June 2025". (Content, SXO, Local)
21. NL pages mix English: service sub-items ("Positioning Naming Brand Voice", "Colours & Typography"), 3 of 6 testimonials, H2 labels. EN homepage shows 3 untranslated Dutch testimonials. (Content)
22. CreativeWork blocks are thin: `dateCreated: "2026"`, `about` as a merged string, no client/sponsor, no `mainEntityOfPage`, no `inLanguage` on homepage, Person, ContactPage or FAQ blocks. No WebSite node. (Schema)
23. Local schema gaps: no `postalCode` (9000), `hasMap`, `contactPoint` with `availableLanguage`; NL schema still says "Ghent". Keep ProfessionalService, drop the redundant LocalBusiness token, do not invent opening hours. (Schema, Local)
24. "+" accordion glyphs leak into FAQ H3 text ("...logo design?+"). Mark aria-hidden. Rotating `#worksWord` span ships as the static H2 "Make them feel grounded". (GEO)
25. Newsletter form is JS-only with no action and no `name` on the input. No llms.txt, no Content-Signal, no .well-known discovery files. (Agentic)
26. Google Fonts stylesheet with 4 weights is render-blocking; self-host Inter. Logo is a 37 KB JPG loaded eagerly three times. (Performance)

### Low

27. Identical `lastmod` 2026-06-07 on all 34 URLs.
28. `http://www` redirects in two hops.
29. Generic Netlify 404 page, no nav, no NL variant. No favicon (`/favicon.ico` 404, no `<link rel=icon>`).
30. Relative asset paths (`../styles-v2.css`) break if a page is ever served at a different depth. Use root-relative paths.
31. Spaces in filenames (`LOGO ICON-02 2.jpg`).
32. No skip link, no `<header>` landmark, duplicated nav in DOM.
33. hreflang declares only `nl-BE`; consider adding `nl`.
34. robots.txt named AI bot blocks are redundant with the wildcard, and those bots skip the `/index-v1.html` disallow (which is 404 anyway).
35. **Info:** FAQPage schema on 5 page types x 2 languages. Google retired FAQ rich results for all sites on 2026-05-07. Harmless, no SERP benefit, do not add more, no need to remove.
